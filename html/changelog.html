24 May 2026

s950tx16wasr10 updated:

  • Lavaland bombs can now go up to 10x the station cap instead of 2x.
  • Atmospherics simulator console in the toxins lab. Two modes: TTV Bomb (preview tank transfer valve bombs without detonating one) and Gas Reactions (sandbox single-tank gas reactions). Trash can at the east wall of toxins is replaced with two reinforced tables, the console sits to the left of the existing east camera.
  • low-pop bonus package. At roundstart, if active players < 7, the station gets a one-shot supply drop (uranium, titanium, bluespace crystals, diamond, plasma, bluespace miner boards, tier-4 stock parts) plus tier-3 cloners, 1.5x R&D speed, doubled department budgets, and tripled paychecks.
  • new "Force Low-Pop Bonus" verb under the Server category for testing.
  • tachyon-doppler array and every other COMSIG_GLOB_EXPLOSION listener now actually receive explosion events during normal play. Previously the signal only fired when an admin had toggled the DebugGame verb on, so all listeners were silently dead under regular gameplay.
  • the tachyon-doppler array now speaks the explosion report for every detonation it picks up. Previously it only spoke when the bomb had been capped by the explosion-radius limit; uncapped bombs went silent (though the on-screen log was still populated).
  • CI no longer double-fires the test, compile_maps, and lint workflows on every push to an open PR.
  • low-pop bonus DNA scanner and cloner pod parts now actually upgrade in place. Previously the upgraded parts were placed in the machine's contents, which made them spill out of the scanner when opened, and the wholesale parts wipe also deleted the circuit board, cable coil, and glass sheet the machine needs for deconstruction.
  • server status now reports as "ReduxStation" instead of "/tg/ Station 13"
